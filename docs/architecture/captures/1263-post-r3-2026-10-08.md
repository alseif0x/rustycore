## Instrumentation

All instrumentation is in production paths, adds no lock, and performs no
await/I/O inside a guard. Every emission site copies its record **under** the
guard and emits **after** the guard is released.

C1:

| Point | Anchor |
|---|---|
| Admission predicate (verdict + deciding clause) | `crates/wow-world-core/src/session/creature_canonical_adapter.rs:214` (`creature_representation_is_admitted_like_cpp`), delegating to `:228` (`creature_representation_admission_decision_like_cpp`) |
| Incarnation identity accessor | `crates/wow-entities/src/unit/state.rs:462` (`timeline_identity_like_cpp`) |
| Refusal + mutation/application point | `crates/wow-world-core/src/session/world_entities/creature_registry.rs:267` (`with_admitted_world_creature_like_cpp`) and its guarded body `:286` (`with_admitted_world_creature_captured_like_cpp`) |
| Shared mirror root (the root the live melee/movement mirrors actually use) | `crates/wow-world-core/src/session/creature_canonical_adapter.rs:524` (`sync_admitted_creature_representation_on_map_like_cpp`) and its guarded body `:558` |
| Single emission point | `crates/wow-world-core/src/session/creature_canonical_adapter.rs:153` (`emit_creature_representation_capture_like_cpp`) |

**Declared addition.** The contract names the admission predicate and
`with_admitted_world_creature_like_cpp`. The live player-melee path does **not**
go through that root: `run_legacy_player_melee_tick_once_like_cpp` mutates the
legacy representation and then mirrors it through
`sync_admitted_creature_representation_on_map_like_cpp`. That second root was
therefore instrumented with the same predicate and the same capture record;
without it the live path would emit nothing.

**Emission levels (declared).** One event is emitted per decision, at the level
its content deserves. The two mirroring roots evaluate the predicate for every
creature of the player's map on every tick, so the routine case — admitted,
applied, no mutation invoked and carrying exactly the revision the incarnation
already holds — is a `DEBUG` event; a decision that refuses, that carries a
newer representation revision, or that ran the mutation root's callback is an
`INFO` event, which is the level the server runs at. This was measured: the
first candidate revision emitted all 519 902 routine mirror decisions at `INFO`
in ~5 minutes (382 MB of log, 4 817 `world-phase pass is past its deadline`
warnings — i.e. the instrumentation was starving the tick loop). After the level
split the same traffic produced **zero** deadline warnings and exactly **20**
`INFO` C1 events over the whole campaign.

is reported as *not observed* together with the reason, never as an absent map.

### C1 — injected stale/ABA replay (harness evidence)

The replay capture is written by the harness when
`RUSTYCORE_C1_REPLAY_CAPTURE` is set and holds exactly three events, one per
decision, each drained immediately after its call (artifact sha256

| ABA | `false` | `revision` | not invoked | `admission_refused` | **matches** |

The ABA row is the point of the capture: the canonical health tuple returned to
its previous values while the revision advanced (`canonical_health_revision=4`,
`representation_health_revision=2`, `health_tuple_matches=true`), and the
predicate still refused the replay on the revision clause instead of mistaking it
for a fresh snapshot. Every refusal event also carries
`shares_health_timeline=true`, which distinguishes a stale/ABA replay of one
incarnation's snapshot from a foreign incarnation. The harness additionally
asserts, in the same test, that both refusals returned `None` from the
production root and that the callback was never invoked. **This is injected
harness evidence.**

## Claims closed

Nothing about R6 or R7 parity is claimed. The claims this record supports are
narrow:

1. The production admission predicate and the mutation/application root are
   instrumented, and the instrumentation is exercised by a real running server
   under real client traffic (C1 live window above).
2. The admission decision record carries owner GUID, incarnation/health-timeline
   identity, verdict, deciding clause and whether the representation was
   applied, and is copied under the guards and emitted after they are released.
3. The stale and ABA refusal paths of the same production root decide on the
   `revision` clause and apply nothing — established by the **injected** replay,
   not by production observation.
4. The login attach resolves the canonical map key and reports it (C2 live).
5. The legacy map-key fallback and the legacy map presence are instrumented and
   report their decision; at the login attach the presence read is blocked by the
   legacy manager guard and is reported as *not observed* with that reason.

## Remaining holds

- **No naturally observed production rejection.** The audit row on whether the
  health-revision/ABA entity-replacement rejection fires in production and
  reaches a client is answered *negatively for this campaign*: it did not fire in
  ten combat attempts, and the refusal evidence is injected. The row is closed
  with the C1 capture ID for the part that was exercised, with that negative
  result written down; no affirmative production rejection is claimed.
- **No naturally observed health-revision mutation.** The combat scenario could
  not damage the target with the available fixture (level 3 versus level 60), so
  the revision-advancing mutation is covered only by the instrumented
  attack-start mutation and by the injected replay.
- **The detached/far-transfer window remains unexercised.** The bot's only
  far-transfer producer is its LFG dungeon-teleport flow (five bots, group and
  LFG proposal, `CMSG_DF_TELEPORT` → `SMSG_NEW_WORLD`); the available fixture
  characters are levels 3/80/80/80/3 and cannot queue for the configured dungeon,
  so that flow was not run. Consequently the audit rows for D-10 runtime
  reachability, for a D-03 fallback caller executing while the canonical key is
  absent, and for the legacy `(map_id, 0)` map in the detached windows stay open.
- **The holder of the legacy manager guard across the login attach is not
  identified** (re-entrant `try_read` versus a concurrent writer). Open.
- **The emission-level split is part of the instrumentation, not of the
  behaviour under test.** It was necessary to stop the instrumentation from
  starving the world tick loop; the routine mirror decision is only visible at
  `DEBUG`.
- R6 itself remains a bounded hold (see plan §8.1 entry, verbatim sentence).
